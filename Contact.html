Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Q: How quickly will you respond?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. Emergency concerns are prioritized.

Q: Is my information secure?

Absolutely. We take data protection seriously and comply with all relevant privacy regulations. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Q: Can I report cyberbullying anonymously?

Yes, you can report safety concerns anonymously. However, providing contact information helps us follow up if needed.

Q: Do you offer workshops or training?

Yes! We offer workshops for schools, parents, and teens. Contact us for more information about scheduling.

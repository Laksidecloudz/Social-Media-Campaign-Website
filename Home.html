Stay Safe Online

Essential tips for navigating the digital world with confidence:

1

Think Before You Share

Consider the privacy implications before posting anything online. Once something is online, it can be difficult to erase.

2

Limit Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information like your address, phone number, or full name on social media.

3

Use Strong Passwords

Create unique and complex passwords for each of your online accounts. Enable two-factor authentication when available.

More Safety Essentials

⚠️
Be Wary of Scams

Be cautious of unsolicited messages or requests for money.

🚨
Report Suspicious Activity

Report inappropriate content to the platform or authorities.

Trust Your Instincts

If something feels wrong, it probably is. Trust your gut.

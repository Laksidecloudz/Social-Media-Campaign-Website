Tips for Safe Livestreaming

Essential guidelines to protect yourself and your audience while streaming.

1

Create Unique Stream Titles

Choose a unique and creative stream title to make your livestream stand out. Avoid using personal information in titles and keep them appropriate for all audiences.

2

Ensure Proper Lighting

Always make sure your livestreaming area is in a well-lit space with good lighting to ensure clear visuals. Good lighting also helps you see your chat and equipment clearly.

3

Set Clear Boundaries

Post a stream schedule on your profile or in your livestream's chat. Set clear boundaries about what topics are off-limits and what behavior won't be tolerated in your community.

4

Protect Personal Information

Never disclose personal or private information while livestreaming, even if it seems harmless. Always think twice before sharing any personal details or posting sensitive content.

5

Engage Your Audience

Ask your audience to interact through the chat function, or incorporate a Q&A session to create an interactive and engaging experience for your viewers while keeping conversations appropriate.

6

Moderate Your Chat

Use moderation tools available on your platform. Set up chat filters, appoint trusted moderators, and don't hesitate to timeout or ban users who violate your community guidelines.

Advanced Safety Features

Take your streaming safety to the next level with these platform-specific features.

🛡️

AutoMod & Chat Filters

Enable automatic moderation to filter inappropriate language, links, and spam before they appear in your chat.

👥

Trusted Moderators

Appoint trusted community members as moderators to help manage chat while you focus on streaming.

🔒

Subscriber-Only Mode

Limit chat to subscribers or followers only to reduce trolling and create a safer community space.

⏸️

Stream Delay

Add a delay to your stream to prevent stream sniping and give moderators time to remove inappropriate content.

🎯 Streamer Safety Checklist

⚠️ Remember:

Never share your real name, address, school, or other identifying information while live. Predators can use this information to find you.

Popular Apps Guide

Explore safety tips and techniques for navigating popular social media platforms responsibly.

🔍
𝕏

Twitter / X

Real-time conversations & news

Twitter is a dynamic platform where users share thoughts and engage in conversations. Safety on Twitter involves managing privacy settings to control who can interact with your tweets.

  • Be cautious about sharing personal information publicly
  • Report any abusive or harassing behavior immediately
  • Avoid clicking on suspicious links
  • Be skeptical of unverified information
  • Think before you tweet to maintain a positive online presence
💡 Pro Tip:

Use the "Protect your Tweets" feature to make your account private and control who sees your content.

f

Facebook

Connecting with friends & family

As one of the largest social networks, Facebook offers various safety features. Protect your account by enabling two-factor authentication and regularly reviewing your privacy settings.

  • Enable two-factor authentication for extra security
  • Regularly review your privacy settings
  • Be mindful of what you share, especially personal information
  • Adjust your audience settings for posts
  • Avoid engaging with suspicious accounts
  • Report inappropriate content to Facebook's support team
💡 Pro Tip:

Use Facebook's Privacy Checkup tool to review and update your privacy settings regularly.

📷

Instagram

Visual storytelling & photos

Instagram is popular for sharing photos and videos, but safety is essential. Set your account to private to control who can view your posts and stories.

  • Set your account to private for better control
  • Be cautious when interacting with unknown users
  • Avoid sharing sensitive information publicly
  • Report abusive or inappropriate comments or messages
  • Use strong, unique passwords
  • Enable login notifications for added security
💡 Pro Tip:

Use the "Close Friends" feature for stories you only want specific people to see.

👻

Snapchat

Ephemeral messaging & stories

Snapchat emphasizes ephemeral messaging, but safety remains a priority. Only accept friend requests from people you know in real life and regularly review your privacy settings.

  • Only accept friend requests from people you know
  • Regularly review your privacy settings
  • Avoid sharing your location with strangers
  • Be cautious about opening snaps from unknown users
  • Report any instances of bullying or harassment
  • Remember: Screenshots can be taken without your knowledge
💡 Pro Tip:

Enable "Ghost Mode" in Snap Map to hide your location from all users.

💬

WhatsApp

Secure messaging & calls

WhatsApp prioritizes user privacy and security with end-to-end encryption. Protect your chats by enabling two-step verification and avoiding sharing sensitive information in group chats.

  • Enable two-step verification for your account
  • Avoid sharing sensitive information in group chats
  • Be cautious about clicking links from unknown contacts
  • Verify the identity of the sender before responding
  • Report suspicious activity or spam messages
  • Regularly update the app for latest security features
💡 Pro Tip:

Check the security code when starting a new chat to verify end-to-end encryption is active.

♪

TikTok

Short-form video content

TikTok has become hugely popular with teens for creative expression. Ensure your account is set to private, be mindful of what you share, and use the Family Pairing feature for additional safety.

  • Set your account to private to control who sees your videos
  • Disable comments or filter them for inappropriate content
  • Be mindful of personal information visible in your videos
  • Use Family Pairing to link with a parent's account
  • Report bullying or inappropriate content quickly
  • Take breaks and manage your screen time
💡 Pro Tip:

Enable "Restricted Mode" to filter out content that may not be appropriate.

▶

YouTube

Video sharing & streaming

YouTube is the world's largest video platform. Create a safe experience by managing your privacy settings, being careful with comments, and using Restricted Mode to filter content.

  • Keep your subscriptions and liked videos private
  • Disable comments on your own videos or hold them for review
  • Be cautious about sharing personal details in video descriptions
  • Use Restricted Mode to filter potentially mature content
  • Report videos that violate community guidelines
  • Don't engage with negative or hateful commenters
💡 Pro Tip:

Create playlists of your favorite content and set them to private.

📺

Twitch

Live streaming platform

Twitch is the leading platform for live streaming gaming and creative content. Protect yourself by moderating your chat, being careful with personal information, and understanding the platform's safety tools.

  • Use AutoMod to filter inappropriate chat messages
  • Set up chat rules and ban words/phrases
  • Be cautious about revealing personal information while live
  • Report harassment or inappropriate behavior immediately
  • Use the Block and Ignore features as needed
  • Take regular breaks from streaming and viewing
💡 Pro Tip:

Enable Follower-Only or Subscriber-Only chat modes to limit who can message you.

Universal Safety Tips

Apply these rules across all social media platforms for maximum safety.

🔐

Strong Passwords

Use unique, complex passwords for each platform. Consider a password manager.

🚫

Block & Report

Don't hesitate to block users who make you uncomfortable and report violations.

⏰

Time Limits

Set daily time limits for social media use to maintain a healthy balance.

👨‍👩‍👧

Talk to Parents

Keep communication open with trusted adults about your online experiences.

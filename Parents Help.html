How Parents Can Help

Discover top tips for supporting healthy teen use of social media and ensuring online safety.

1

Stay Informed

Parents can stay informed about social media platforms by actively researching and exploring the apps and websites their teenagers use. This includes understanding the features, privacy settings, and potential risks associated with each platform.

Keeping up-to-date with news articles, online resources, and parenting guides can also provide valuable insights into current trends and issues in the digital world.

📋 Action Items:
  • Create accounts on platforms your teen uses
  • Subscribe to digital parenting newsletters
  • Attend school workshops on online safety
  • Join parent groups focused on teen tech use
2

Set Boundaries

Setting boundaries for healthy social media use is essential for parents. Establish clear guidelines regarding screen time, appropriate content, and online behavior.

Encourage breaks from devices and promote offline activities to maintain balance. Communicate openly with your teenagers about the importance of boundaries and involve them in the process of establishing rules.

📋 Suggested Boundaries:
  • No phones during family meals
  • Devices charged outside bedrooms overnight
  • 2-hour daily screen time limit
  • Approval required for new app downloads
3

Open Communication

Fostering open communication with teens about their online experiences builds trust and facilitates understanding. Encourage regular conversations about social media use, including any challenges or concerns they may encounter.

Create a supportive environment where teenagers feel comfortable sharing their online interactions, friendships, and encounters with potentially harmful content. Listen attentively, validate their experiences, and offer guidance without judgment.

💬 Conversation Starters:
  • "What's the funniest thing you saw online today?"
  • "Have you seen any drama happening on social media?"
  • "Is there anyone online who makes you uncomfortable?"
  • "What would you do if someone was being mean online?"
4

Use Parental Controls

Utilizing parental control tools can provide added safety and oversight for parents. Explore the built-in features of social media platforms, such as privacy settings, content filters, and activity monitoring options.

Additionally, consider installing third-party parental control apps or software that offer comprehensive management tools. Collaborate with your teenager to establish appropriate restrictions that prioritize their safety and well-being.

🛠️ Recommended Tools:
  • Google Family Link (Android)
  • Screen Time (iOS)
  • Bark monitoring service
  • Qustodio parental control

Warning Signs to Watch For

Be aware of these indicators that your teen may be experiencing issues online.

😰

Emotional Changes

Sudden mood swings, anxiety, depression, or withdrawal after using devices may indicate cyberbullying or other online issues.

😴

Sleep Disruption

Difficulty sleeping, nightmares, or staying up extremely late online can signal problematic internet use or online harassment.

📱

Secretive Behavior

Hiding screens, quickly closing apps when you enter, or refusing to discuss online activities may be red flags.

🚫

Avoiding School

Reluctance to go to school or social events may indicate that online conflicts are spilling into real-world interactions.

If you notice these signs, approach your teen with empathy and concern. Contact our support team for guidance.
